MERCHANTPAY LITE
User Roles, Mappings, and User Journeys
1. User Roles
Merchant
●​ Description: Business owners and commercial entities using MerchantPay Lite to securely accept
online payments from their customers for goods and services.
●​ Business Goals: Collect payments promptly, view consolidated payment histories, monitor daily
bank settlements, and quickly resolve payment exceptions to maintain healthy cash flow.
●​ Business Challenges: Multiple external payment integrations leading to fragmented reporting,
delayed fund settlements, high manual reconciliation effort, and poor real-time payment status
visibility.
●​ Business Value: Provides a centralized, secure payment collection framework that streamlines fund
acceptance, improves settlement speed, and automates payment reporting.

Customer
●​ Description: Individual buyers or entity representatives purchasing goods or services provided by
merchants operating on the platform.
●​ Business Goals: Complete checkout transactions quickly, receive immediate verification of payment
completion, and experience a secure payment flow.
●​ Business Challenges: Frequent payment failures, delayed confirmation receipts, unclear error
notices during checkout, and lack of transaction transparency.
●​ Business Value: Delivers a dependable, transparent payment processing environment that ensures
immediate transaction feedback and reduces checkout friction.

Bank Operations Officer
●​ Description: Internal bank personnel responsible for administrative payment oversight, settlement
verification, financial reconciliation, and operational issue resolution.
●​ Business Goals: Maintain oversight of daily payment activities, ensure accurate account
reconciliation, investigate transaction failures, and generate comprehensive operational reports.
●​ Business Challenges: Reliance on manual reconciliation processes, fragmented operational
reporting tools, time-consuming dispute investigations, and high operational overhead costs.
●​ Business Value: Centralizes operational monitoring, reduces manual workload, accelerates
transaction investigations, and improves settlement accuracy.

System Administrator
●​ Description: Internal technical governance personnel responsible for managing overall platform
operations, account provisioning, platform availability, and security compliance.
●​ Business Goals: Maintain high system availability, manage user access permissions, oversee
merchant onboarding, and safeguard platform data security.
●​ Business Challenges: Balancing system scalability with security compliance, managing access rights
safely across diverse user roles, and maintaining comprehensive operational audit logs.
●​ Business Value: Ensures operational governance, system reliability, regulatory compliance, and
scalable platform administration.

Customer Support Representative
●​ Description: Frontline operational personnel tasked with responding to customer and merchant
inquiries, troubleshooting transaction complaints, and providing payment resolution assistance.

●​ Business Goals: Resolve user inquiries quickly, locate transaction records efficiently, provide
accurate payment status updates, and minimize complaint resolution cycles.
●​ Business Challenges: Incomplete transaction visibility, reliance on disparate legacy systems to trace
payment status, and slow inquiry turnaround times.
●​ Business Value: Enables single-point visibility into transaction activity and historical logs, improving
response accuracy and customer satisfaction.

Fraud & Risk Officer
●​ Description: Internal risk management specialists focused on monitoring transaction activity,
preventing fraudulent transactions, managing chargebacks, and mitigating financial risks.
●​ Business Goals: Protect bank and merchant revenue, identify suspicious payment activity early,
enforce risk controls, and minimize financial exposure.
●​ Business Challenges: High transaction volume making manual risk identification difficult,
unauthorized financial activities, potential security breaches, and legal exposure.
●​ Business Value: Delivers automated risk oversight and control capabilities, protecting ecosystem
participants from financial fraud and operational losses.

External Compliance Auditor
●​ Description: Regulatory and compliance oversight authorities responsible for auditing financial
records, system security integrity, operational policies, and statutory compliance.
●​ Business Goals: Verify operational compliance with regulatory guidelines, evaluate financial audit
trails, and ensure systemic data security integrity.
●​ Business Challenges: Gathering comprehensive audit trails from fragmented systems, verifying
compliance across multiple operational processes, and ensuring data integrity.
●​ Business Value: Provides verifiable, transparent audit trails and centralized reporting access to
simplify regulatory reporting and compliance verifications.

Developer / System Integrator
●​ Description: Technical partner teams responsible for connecting merchant enterprise platforms and
e-commerce websites to the payment gateway service.
●​ Business Goals: Integrate merchant systems smoothly, enable automated payment collection, verify
integration reliability, and minimize technical setup delays.
●​ Business Challenges: Managing complex third-party payment connections, addressing technical
integration errors, and adapting to custom business requirements.
●​ Business Value: Streamlines merchant setup, accelerates onboarding timelines, and enables
automated payment acceptance for merchant businesses.

2. Business Responsibilities by Persona
Merchant
●​ Creating Payment Requests: Generating payment requests to initiate secure fund collection for
goods or services offered.
●​ Monitoring Incoming Payments: Tracking customer transaction statuses and fund receipts in real
time.
●​ Reviewing Settlements: Monitoring bank payout schedules, reviewing settlement details, and
verifying payout balances.
●​ Managing Business Information: Maintaining business profile information, updating payment
collection preferences, and ensuring account data accuracy.
●​ Tracking Business Performance: Utilizing transaction data and summary reports to evaluate sales
activity and revenue trends.

Customer
●​ Choosing a Preferred Payment Method: Selecting the payment instrument best suited for
completing the transaction.
●​ Completing Payments: Authorizing and submitting accurate payment information to fulfill
purchasing obligations.
●​ Confirming Payment Information: Reviewing checkout details and verifying transaction receipts
post-purchase.
●​ Keeping Payment Credentials Secure: Safeguarding personal financial identifiers and
authentication credentials during payment execution.

Bank Operations Officer
●​ Monitoring Settlements: Overseeing financial transfer processes to ensure merchant payout
schedules are executed accurately.
●​ Reconciling Payment Records: Cross-referencing transaction activity logs against bank settlement
funds to maintain financial accuracy.
●​ Investigating Transaction Issues: Tracing payment failures, analyzing dispute origins, and guiding
exception resolution processes.
●​ Resolving Settlement Discrepancies: Addressing financial variances, unapplied funds, and
processing delays to balance operational accounts.

System Administrator
●​ Managing Merchant Onboarding: Authorizing new merchant registrations, reviewing profile data,
and activating service privileges.
●​ Governing Platform Access & Permissions: Assigning security roles, managing operational
entitlements, and ensuring appropriate administrative controls.
●​ Ensuring Platform Health & Security: Overseeing system reliability, maintaining platform operational
capabilities, and enforcing data security standards.
●​ Maintaining Audit Logs: Preserving comprehensive operational activity logs to satisfy regulatory
requirements and operational reviews.

Customer Support Representative
●​ Locating Transaction Records: Researching customer and merchant payment records to address
operational inquiries.
●​ Analyzing Failure Causes: Reviewing transaction activity logs to identify causes of payment
interruptions or delays.
●​ Assisting Inquiry Resolution: Guiding merchants and customers toward resolving payment
discrepancies and re-issuing transaction notices.

Fraud & Risk Officer
●​ Monitoring Flagged Transactions: Reviewing suspicious or high-risk transaction activities against
institutional risk criteria.
●​ Enforcing Operational Risk Controls: Temporarily holding payouts or placing restrictions on
accounts flagged for security review.
●​ Mitigating Financial Risk: Managing chargeback disputes, investigating unauthorized transaction
attempts, and limiting institutional liability.

External Compliance Auditor
●​ Reviewing System Audit Trails: Inspecting system usage records, administrative changes, and
financial processing trails.
●​ Verifying Regulatory Compliance: Assessing gateway operations against industry financial

standards, data security norms, and legal rules.
●​ Exporting Compliance Data: Extracting financial and security operational logs for formal regulatory
submission and governance reviews.

Developer / System Integrator
●​ Establishing Technical Integration: Connecting merchant e-commerce platforms to payment
collection services.
●​ Verifying Payment Processing Workflows: Validating that merchant platforms correctly initiate
payments and receive status updates.
●​ Maintaining Connection Security: Ensuring API authentication credentials and callback
configurations adhere to security practices.

3. Business User Journeys
Merchant User Journey
●​ Business Need: The merchant needs an efficient way to accept digital payments from customers,
eliminate payment collection bottlenecks, track incoming revenues, and streamline bank account
settlements.
●​ Business Process:
○​ Onboarding & Access: The merchant completes business profile setup, verifies account
credentials, and accesses the payment administration interface.
○​ Payment Request Generation: The merchant creates digital payment requests to facilitate
transactions for sales opportunities.
○​ Real-Time Oversight: The merchant tracks sales activity as customer payments are processed,
monitoring real-time completion statuses.
○​ Reconciliation & Reporting: The merchant reviews daily transaction summaries and settlement
reports to verify that funds are transferred accurately to their bank account.
○​ Exception Resolution: In cases where payments fail, the merchant reviews transaction details to
identify the cause and assist the customer in completing the transaction.
●​ Expected Business Outcome: Accelerated cash collection, reduced manual reconciliation effort,
complete payment visibility, and dependable fund settlements.

Customer User Journey
●​ Business Need: The customer wants a simple, intuitive, and trustworthy checkout process to pay for
goods or services online without operational errors or uncertainty.
●​ Business Process:
○​ Checkout Initiation: The customer initiates the purchase process by selecting their preferred
payment method.
○​ Payment Fulfillment: The customer provides the necessary payment authorization details to
complete the purchase.
○​ Verification & Confirmation: The system processes the payment, delivering immediate
notification confirming whether the transaction was successful or encountered an issue.
●​ Expected Business Outcome: A secure checkout experience, immediate confirmation of
transaction completion, and minimal purchase friction.

Bank Operations Officer User Journey
●​ Business Need: The Operations Officer requires unified operational visibility to oversee daily
merchant payment activities, resolve operational exceptions, and ensure accurate financial
reconciliation.
●​ Business Process:
○​ Daily Operational Monitoring: The officer monitors daily processing volumes across all active

merchants on the network.
○​ Transaction Investigation: The officer investigates payment failures and merchant payout
inquiries using transaction event histories.
○​ Reconciliation Management: The officer matches platform payment totals with bank settlement
batches, identifying and resolving any financial discrepancies.
○​ Operational Reporting: The officer generates daily settlement logs and summary reports for
executive leadership and internal audit teams.
●​ Expected Business Outcome: Efficient operational oversight, streamlined financial reconciliation,
reduced support escalation resolution times, and accurate merchant payouts.

System Administrator User Journey
●​ Business Need: The Administrator requires centralized management capabilities to provision users,
control operational access, and maintain data security, availability, and regulatory compliance.
●​ Business Process:
○​ Merchant Provisioning: The administrator reviews and approves new merchant access,
activating operational payment privileges.
○​ Access Governance: The administrator assigns user roles, reviews operational permissions, and
maintains access security boundaries across all organizational roles.
○​ System Oversight: The administrator checks system activity metrics and performance health
indicators to ensure continuous processing availability.
○​ Audit Log Management: The administrator monitors operational logging processes to preserve
comprehensive records for financial and security compliance requirements.
●​ Expected Business Outcome: Controlled system access, sustained high availability, robust
operational security, and complete audit readiness.

Customer Support Representative User Journey
●​ Business Need: The Support Representative needs efficient lookup tools to investigate merchant
and customer transaction issues, explain failure causes, and resolve customer complaints promptly.
●​ Business Process:
○​ Inquiry Intake: The representative receives a customer or merchant inquiry regarding an
unconfirmed payment or settlement delay.
○​ Information Retrieval: The representative searches for the payment using relevant customer or
business reference identifiers.
○​ Status & Error Analysis: The representative evaluates transaction step logs to determine the
exact cause of processing interruptions.
○​ Issue Resolution: The representative provides status clarity, triggers updated transaction
notifications, or escalates complex operational issues to Bank Operations.
●​ Expected Business Outcome: Faster inquiry resolution, improved customer satisfaction, reduced
operational confusion, and consistent communication.

Fraud & Risk Officer User Journey
●​ Business Need: The Risk Officer needs visibility into payment patterns to safeguard platform
transactions, prevent fraud losses, and ensure risk compliance.
●​ Business Process:
○​ Risk Queue Monitoring: The officer monitors automated risk indicators to identify abnormal
payment patterns or high-risk transactions.
○​ Transaction Investigation: The officer analyzes transaction details, merchant activity trends, and
payment attempts to determine potential risk exposure.
○​ Risk Control Enforcement: The officer places temporary holds on flagged merchant payouts or
suspicious transactions while conducting further safety verifications.
●​ Expected Business Outcome: Reduced risk of financial fraud, protection of institutional reputation,

and reduced chargeback exposure.

External Compliance Auditor User Journey
●​ Business Need: The Compliance Auditor requires verified access to operational trails, transaction
logs, and financial settlement records to confirm statutory compliance.
●​ Business Process:
○​ Compliance Review Initiation: The auditor specifies the scope and timeframes for regulatory
inspection.
○​ Audit Trail Examination: The auditor inspects read-only system logs, user access activities, and
settlement balances.
○​ Data Extraction & Verification: The auditor exports structured activity logs to verify operational
accuracy against industry compliance standards.
●​ Expected Business Outcome: Transparent compliance reviews, validated operational reporting, and
verification of institutional governance standards.

Developer / System Integrator User Journey
●​ Business Need: The Integrator needs a reliable integration process to link merchant systems with the
payment gateway, enabling automated payment processing.
●​ Business Process:
○​ Integration Setup: The integrator obtains system connection credentials and configures
payment settings according to business needs.
○​ Workflow Validation: The integrator tests payment workflows, payment status messaging, and
transaction response handling.
○​ Production Activation: The integrator enables the payment integration on live merchant sales
platforms to allow customer transactions.
●​ Expected Business Outcome: Efficient merchant deployment, dependable payment integration, and
automated business payment collection.
